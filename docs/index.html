ML · CAPSTONE

CTR / Engagement Opportunity Scoring

Problem. A page can have search visibility without capturing clicks efficiently. A reviewer needs a way to prioritize investigation without treating low CTR as proof that a page is broken.

What I did. I framed the problem as opportunity scoring, built a time-aware feature/label setup over the FlyRank warehouse, checked leakage, compared a transparent baseline with a model, and produced ranked recommendations with reason codes.

What came of it. A repeatable decision-support workflow: rank pages for review, explain why they surfaced, and keep the recommendation human-reviewed.

Prompt engineering on real work

Problem. Generic prompts produced generic answers.

What I did. I used a six-run ladder, changing one technique at a time and comparing outputs.

Outcome. A reusable prompting workflow based on observed improvements.

ML FOUNDATIONS

Data contract to baseline

Problem. Modeling without a data contract makes leakage and unclear decision points easy to miss.

What I did. Defined grain, time window, target/proxy, availability checks, decision-time features and a deliberate leakage test.

Outcome. A clearer row → signal → score → action chain.
